FAQ

Questions dealers ask

What is TractionOmni?

TractionOmni is a market intelligence service for dealers. It shows your market share, who is gaining ground, and where demand is going unserved, by brand, segment and geography.

Who is it for?

Dealers and dealer groups, starting with boat dealers. Powersports, RV and trailer dealers are next. Manufacturers can contact us about brand and network views.

What does the free snapshot include?

Your share by brand and segment in your market, how it changed from last year, and a short list of white space opportunities near your store.

Is it really free?

Yes, for founding dealers. We are working closely with a small number of dealers in each market before launch. Paid plans with full access come later.

Where does the data come from?

From official and licensed industry sources, verified and standardized by our team. We report counts of units, never personal information about buyers.

Do you share my information?

No. Your details are used only to prepare your snapshot and to contact you. We never sell your information.
